Sources. Baseline vote totals for the 29 September 2024 National Council election, nationally, by state and by regional constituency, come from the certified result published by the Austrian Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI). The number of seats per state follows the apportionment set out in the Federal Ministry of the Interior's ordinance BGBl. II 2023/180. Map outlines are derived from the official composition of the constituencies (BMI) and OpenStreetMap-based district boundaries from the ginseng666/GeoJSON-TopoJSON-Austria project (CC-BY-SA, OpenStreetMap contributors).
